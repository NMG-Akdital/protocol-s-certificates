Accreditations & approvals

Logos of awarding bodies that have approved Protocol S. Each one prints only on certificates for the courses it covers, together with its approval number, and is shown on the verification page.
Before adding an approval
  • Use the official logo file and the exact wording from your approval letter or the body's brand guidelines (for example, the approved-provider mark).
  • Select only the courses the approval covers. Approvals are usually course-specific, so most of your in-house courses should not carry the logo.
  • Set the approval's expiry date, and the logo stops printing automatically after it. Certificates already issued keep what they were issued with.
